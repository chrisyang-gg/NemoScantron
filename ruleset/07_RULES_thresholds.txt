COMPOSITE SCORE FORMULA
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  composite = (
    (velocity_behavioral_score  ×  0.25) +
    (geo_device_score           ×  0.35) +
    (merchant_auth_score        ×  0.30) +
    (account_context_boost      ×  0.10)
  )

  final_risk_score = MIN(1.000,  composite + pattern_match_boost)

Where:
  velocity_behavioral_score  = clamped sum of all fired VB rule contributions (FILE 3)
  geo_device_score           = clamped sum of all fired GEO rule contributions (FILE 4)
  merchant_auth_score        = clamped sum of all fired MA rule contributions (FILE 5)
  account_context_boost      = 0.10 if VB-006 fires; 0.15 if VB-007 fires; else 0.00
                               (if both fire, use the higher value, not the sum)
  pattern_match_boost        = the single highest pattern_risk_boost from FILE 6
                               (boosts are NOT additive; use MAX, not SUM)

SECTION WEIGHT RATIONALE
  Geo/device (0.35) is highest because physical impossibility and device anomalies
  are very hard for legitimate cardholders to accidentally trigger and very hard
  for attackers to suppress.
  Merchant/auth (0.30) is second because authentication failures are direct evidence
  of unauthorized access, not probabilistic inference.
  Velocity/behavioral (0.25) carries lower weight because many legitimate users
  also show elevated velocity in travel or shopping seasons.
  Account context (0.10) is a passive modifier, not a primary signal.

DECISION BOUNDARY TABLE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  final_risk_score < 0.200              →  decision: approve
  0.200 <= final_risk_score < 0.500     →  decision: flag_for_review
  0.500 <= final_risk_score < 0.750     →  decision: hold
  final_risk_score >= 0.750             →  decision: decline

  HARD OVERRIDE CONDITIONS (applied after threshold mapping, cannot be lowered):

    a. GEO-001 fires AND implied speed > 900 km/h
       → minimum decision: hold  (regardless of composite score)

    b. MA-005 (CVV mismatch) AND MA-007 (AVS full mismatch) BOTH fire
       → minimum decision: flag_for_review  (rarely approve)

    c. ACCOUNT-TAKEOVER pattern matched
       → minimum decision: hold

    d. Any rule with analyst_escalation_required == true fires
       → escalate_to_analyst: true  (regardless of decision level)

    e. account.account_status IN ["suspended", "delinquent"]
       → minimum decision: hold  (regardless of composite score)

ESCALATE_TO_ANALYST RULES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  escalate_to_analyst = true   when:
    - decision is "flag_for_review" (always)
    - decision is "hold" (always)
    - decision is "decline" AND any triggered rule/pattern carries
      analyst_escalation_required == true

  escalate_to_analyst = false  when:
    - decision is "approve"
    - decision is "decline" AND no triggered rule/pattern carries
      analyst_escalation_required

CONFIDENCE SCORE FORMULA
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  confidence = MAX(0.400,  1.000 - (missing_optional_field_count × 0.040))

Where missing_optional_field_count is the count of absent optional fields across
the velocity_features, location, and authentication blocks, as recorded in
Execution Workflow Step 1 (FILE 2).

The floor of 0.400 ensures that even a highly incomplete record yields a
usable confidence value rather than an implausibly low one.
